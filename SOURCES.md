## Disclaimer

This documentation represents compiled information about the Mongolian Death Worm from various sources including traditional Mongolian accounts, expedition reports, and cryptozoological research. The death worm remains an unconfirmed cryptid—no physical evidence has been scientifically verified despite multiple searches.

Local Mongolian accounts should be treated with respect and cultural sensitivity. These represent genuine beliefs and traditional knowledge within Mongolian desert communities.

For scientific information on Gobi Desert fauna, consult peer-reviewed zoological and herpetological research. For cryptozoological investigation, consult expedition reports and cryptozoology organizations while maintaining ethical research standards.
